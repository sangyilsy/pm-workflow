# AA Product Opportunity Engine

A product discovery and prioritization skill for an Age Assurance PM.

## Goal

Transform unstructured internal evidence into:

**evidence → problems → hypotheses → opportunities → prioritized solutions → validation plan**

The skill is intentionally broader than failure-rate analysis. It considers prevention, UX, policy, assurance, fraud detection, cost optimization, operations, and reusable platform capabilities as potential intervention points.

## Key capabilities

- Combines quantitative, qualitative, and contextual evidence.
- Separates product-addressable failures from policy, fraud, system, and ambiguous cases without assuming any category has no opportunity.
- Builds explicit hypotheses with supporting/contradicting evidence and confidence.
- Identifies what supplemental data would confirm or refute a hypothesis and tells the PM what to collect.
- Generates multiple solution archetypes before prioritizing.
- Accounts explicitly for AA safety, fraud, privacy, compliance, accessibility, and operational risk.
- Cross-checks product proposals against the **Age Assurance Strategic Intelligence** skill for external regulatory, competitor, technology, and market evidence.
