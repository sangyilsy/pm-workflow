# Age Assurance Strategic Intelligence

Strategic intelligence and product decision-support skill for a social-platform Age Assurance PM.

## Goal

Monitor global regulation, competitors, emerging age-assurance technology, and youth-safety policy drivers — then translate developments into actionable product-strategy insights.

The skill also supports day-to-day product inquiries by evaluating internal proposals against regulatory evidence, public industry practice, AA assurance characteristics, UX/privacy/operational trade-offs, and credible alternatives.

## Coverage

- Global age-assurance and teen-safety regulation, with emphasis on U13/U16/U18.
- Major social, gaming, and AI-platform competitor moves.
- Age declaration, estimation, inference, verification, and combined/waterfall approaches.
- Digital identity, credentials, proof-of-age, and privacy-preserving verification.
- Product implications across account, content & discovery, feature/capability, and access/relationship policies.
- User journeys including age gates, verification, age-based experiences, restrictions, correction/challenge, re-verification, parental flows, and underage reporting.
- Youth-safety, child-rights, social, political, and market drivers behind regulatory changes.
- Structured product decision support for questions such as whether a proposed evidence type (e.g. school ID) should be accepted for age verification.
