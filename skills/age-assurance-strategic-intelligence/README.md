# Age Assurance Strategic Intelligence

Strategic intelligence skill for a social-platform Age Assurance PM.

## Goal

Monitor global regulation, competitors, and emerging age-assurance technology, then translate developments into product-strategy and decision-support insights.

## Coverage

- Global age-assurance and teen-safety regulation, with emphasis on U13/U16/U18.
- Major social, gaming, and AI-platform competitor moves.
- Age inference, age estimation, age verification, digital identity, and privacy-preserving proof-of-age technology.
- Product implications across signup, account, content/LIVE, reporting, enforcement, appeals, and ongoing reassessment.
