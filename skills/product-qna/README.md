# Product Q&A

Stakeholder-facing product knowledge layer built on the shared Product Context.

## Primary audience

Customer Support, Operations, Business/partnerships, Legal, Policy, Government Relations, PR/communications, Sales, Engineering, and other stakeholders.

## Purpose

Answer questions about how the product works, what is live, what changed, what is planned, who uses it, and what happened historically — with strong factuality, freshness, and provenance.

See [`SKILL.md`](./SKILL.md) for the operating specification.
